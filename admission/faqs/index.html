Admission

FAQs

Answers to common questions about admissions and school life.

Below are answers to the questions parents most often ask about admissions and day-to-day school life at SKS World School, Greater Noida West. For anything not covered here, please call the admission helpline or write to us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents Ask Us

What is the admission process?

Parents register the child online on the school website for the age-appropriate class and submit the required documents. A written test is conducted, followed by a Parents–Principal interaction. Admission is confirmed on payment of fees.

Does the school provide transport?

Yes. All buses are new, with routes customised to reduce travel time, and every bus is mobile-connected and equipped with GPS and CCTV.

Is there an entrance exam for admission?

For pre-primary, there is a small written test along with an interview with the Principal. For senior classes (Class I onwards), entrance tests are held in English, Mathematics, Science and Hindi.

How many students are there per class?

25 students in Kindergarten, 30 in Classes I–II, and 35 from Class III to Class XII.

Are meals provided?

Meals are available at the canteen on a pay-and-buy basis.

What board does the school follow?

The school follows the CBSE curriculum and is affiliated to CBSE.

Does the school conduct outbound trips?

Yes, outbound trips are organised annually. They are optional, and the expenses are borne by parents.

What is SKS World School's approach to education?

A holistic, student-centred approach — “students learn by doing.”

Whom do I contact for admissions?

Parents can email the school or register online. Contact and phone details are on the Contact page.

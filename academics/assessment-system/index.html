Student performance is assessed through a combination of internal assessment (day-to-day evaluation through assignments, seminar presentations, case studies, practical proficiency, and written tests) and end-of-semester university examinations covering theory and practical/viva components.

What Counts Toward Internal Assessment

Internal assessment is based on day-to-day evaluation of how students participate in the learning process. This includes assignments, seminar preparation, case presentations, case studies and problem-solving exercises, participation in community health projects, and demonstrated proficiency in a practical skill or small research project.

Every student is expected to be punctual and to attend school regularly. A student recovering from a contagious illness must produce a doctor’s certificate of fitness before attending classes.

Leave may be allowed by the Principal on a written application from the parent, either through the mobile app or in writing to the school. A half-day leave is not permitted, for security reasons. In an emergency requiring early departure, the guardian must obtain prior approval from the administration, come to school in person to collect the student, and produce a valid document.

A minimum attendance of 75% in theory and 80% in practical/lab sessions is required for eligibility to sit for semester examinations. Students must secure at least 50% marks in university-conducted examinations, assessed separately in theory and practical, to be declared as passed in a subject.

Where a subject includes more than one paper, a student must secure at least 40% marks in each paper individually, along with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate across both papers, to pass that subject.

Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment. The school aims to build a congenial environment in which all members can live and work together in a supportive way, feeling happy, safe and secure.

Violence — mental or physical — racism, stealing and public displays of affection are forbidden, as are breaking school property, trading on campus, possessing forbidden objects, misbehaving towards staff, and using abusive language. Every student reads and signs an undertaking to accept and abide by the school rules at the start of each session. Severe action, in consultation with the Principal and teachers, will be taken against behaviour that disrupts the educational programme, and persistent violations may result in expulsion.

General Rules

Rules & Regulations

  • Wearing the identity card and respective house badge daily is obligatory.
  • Attendance on all national festival days and school functions is compulsory.
  • Students must observe discipline and decorum in their behaviour, in and outside school.
  • Students should never cheat or act unethically — honesty is required at all times.
  • Respect and care for the classroom, furniture and school premises; defacing furniture or walls is penalised.
  • Avoid whistling, shouting or running in corridors; change classrooms quietly and in an orderly manner.
  • Proper uniform is to be worn by all students.
  • Weapons, knives, sharp instruments and non-textbook materials are not allowed.
  • Cameras, mobile phones and similar devices are strictly prohibited on the school premises.
  • Use of scooters and motorbikes by students is strictly prohibited.
  • The school accepts no liability for lost belongings; unclaimed property is deposited at the office.
  • Notices must be read and communicated to parents.
  • The school reserves the right to expel any student whose progress is unsatisfactory or whose conduct is harmful.

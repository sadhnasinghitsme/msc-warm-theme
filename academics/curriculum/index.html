The MSc curriculum at SKS International University is structured to build strong theoretical foundations before moving into specialized, research-oriented coursework. Each program is divided into core theory papers, laboratory/practical training, and a dissertation or research project component in the final semester — following the same integration of classroom learning and hands-on lab work that defines all SKS International University programs.

How the Phases Build on Each Other

SKS-affiliated medical programs follow a phased structure. Phase I — the foundational phase — spans roughly 13 months (preceded by a one-month Foundation Course) and covers core subjects: Human Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Introduction to Community Medicine, and Professional Development (attitude, ethics, and communication). This is the same foundational grounding that anchors all four MSc Medical specializations — Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, and Microbiology — before students move into the more specialized, research-oriented coursework of later phases.

SKS World School is affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi (Affiliation No. 2134098) and follows the NCERT-based CBSE curriculum in English medium. All students participate in a multi-faceted curriculum, because the key to excellence is not solely in academics but in all aspects of school life.

The academic programme is designed to evolve the intellectual stimulation and curiosity, rational thought and maturity of mind of every student — a well-researched, integrated curriculum that is revised based on the changing needs of children and society, with a focus on application, analytical and problem-solving skills.

Beyond the Textbook

Co-Scholastic Areas

Languages

English is the main medium of instruction; Hindi, Sanskrit and French are stressed upon simultaneously.

Computer Education

Offered from Standard III onward as an integral component of the curriculum.

Physical Education

A “Sports for All” approach with basketball, football, tennis, athletics, swimming, cricket and karate.

Arts

Painting, dancing, choral singing and drama, developing aesthetic appreciation and self-expression.

Community Service

Students take part in social awareness and literacy campaigns as part of their learning.

Examinations

All curriculum and examinations are conducted according to CBSE norms. See Session Info.

About SKS

SKS World School, Greater Noida West is an English-medium, co-educational, CBSE curriculum-based school (Affiliation No. 2134098) that runs under the aegis of the SKS Educational & Social Trust, led by its Chairman Shri S.K. Sharma.

Our Upcoming Schools: SKS World School Indirapuram, SKS World School Vrindavan, SKS World School Pari Chowk.

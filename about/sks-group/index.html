About SKS World School

SKS Group of Institutions

The educational group behind SKS World School and its network of institutions.

The secret of joy in work contains one word — excellence. SKSGI has created a benchmark of excellence in the field of education by provisioning state-of-the-art infrastructure and dedicated faculty. The 1980s marked the establishment of the SKS Group; two decades later, the SKS Group is renowned and operational in several parts of the country.

SKSGI is dedicated to training future technocrats and managers, where students not only gain a University degree but also develop cognitive skills for placement with leading industries. SKS has campuses at distinct locations — Durgapur Public School, SKS World School HS-04 and SKS World School HS-01 in Greater Noida West, and SKS World School in Sector-137, Noida.

SKSGI believes that collaboration is the new competition, and that when we work together we can achieve great things. We provide holistic and meaningful education in addition to the pursuit of academic excellence. SKS World School, Greater Noida West is affiliated to CBSE, New Delhi (Affiliation No. 2134098).

About SKS

SKS World School, Greater Noida West is an English-medium, co-educational, CBSE curriculum-based school (Affiliation No. 2134098) that runs under the aegis of the SKS Educational & Social Trust, led by its Chairman Shri S.K. Sharma.

About SKS International University

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide our teaching, research, and patient care.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, rigorous postgraduate medical education that combines strong theoretical foundations with practical laboratory and research training — producing graduates who are scientifically capable, ethically grounded, and ready to contribute to India's healthcare and research ecosystem.

Looking Ahead

Our Vision

To be recognized as a center of excellence in postgraduate medical sciences, continuing the SKS Group's two-decade legacy of building institutions that serve the public good — and to extend that legacy internationally, drawing scholars from across India and beyond.

About SKS

SKS International University is a 50-acre campus in Mathura offering NMC-regulated MSc Medical programs in Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, and Microbiology.

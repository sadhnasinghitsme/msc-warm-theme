Chairman’s Message

A message from our Chairman, Shri S.K. Sharma.

CHAIRMAN
Shri S.K. Sharma Chairman, SKS Group of Institutions contact@skswsgnw.ac.in
“Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think.

It feels delightful to exemplify the endeavour, motivation and striving of the mentors and parents who come forward and join hands for the better growth of students. Staying informed is the vital factor for every walk of life. To keep abreast of the latest technology and developing society, we have directed all our efforts towards bringing up the Chain of SKS World Schools as a Centre of Excellence in Education and nurturing the children of the present into the leaders of tomorrow.

I am pleased to extend my appreciation towards the untiring efforts put in by the Principal, staff, and parents to evolve the school as a real abode of education.

Having faith in the Almighty, I expect consistency in good work and the best spirit, and wish the school to scale new heights of success in the coming years.

With warm regards,

About SKS

SKS World School, Greater Noida West is an English-medium, co-educational, CBSE curriculum-based school (Affiliation No. 2134098) that runs under the aegis of the SKS Educational & Social Trust, led by its Chairman Shri S.K. Sharma.

Our Upcoming Schools: SKS World School Indirapuram, SKS World School Vrindavan, SKS World School Pari Chowk.

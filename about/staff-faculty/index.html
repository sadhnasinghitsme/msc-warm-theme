About SKS World School

Our Teachers

The mentors who guide every child's learning and social growth.

Our People

A Qualified and Caring Faculty

SKS World School has a qualified and competent faculty. Our experienced teachers care for every student in the academic subjects and in their social training, and every faculty member plays a vital role in the holistic development of the child.

Our mentors use a range of methods — individual, group and whole-class teaching — to encourage learning. They not only ensure that students do well in their studies, but also enable them to explore their own interests and abilities. Work is carefully planned on a weekly basis and evaluated daily to ensure steady improvement.

The school continues to re-engineer its curriculum, develop new and different ways of delivering course content, and bring emerging technology into the classroom, so that teaching keeps pace with the needs of children and society.

In the Classroom

How Our Teachers Work

Individual Attention

A teacher–student ratio of 1:20 means each child receives individual care and attention at all times.

Learning by Doing

Lessons are paired with activities and lab work so that students learn concepts by exploring them, not just reading about them.

Rigorous Selection

Teachers are chosen for their educational qualifications, relevant experience, a demonstration lesson and a rigorous interview.

Continuous Training

Adequate teacher training is given to keep the staff updated, and the school continues to bring emerging technology into the classroom.

About SKS

SKS World School, Greater Noida West is an English-medium, co-educational, CBSE curriculum-based school (Affiliation No. 2134098) that runs under the aegis of the SKS Educational & Social Trust, led by its Chairman Shri S.K. Sharma.

Our Upcoming Schools: SKS World School Indirapuram, SKS World School Vrindavan, SKS World School Pari Chowk.

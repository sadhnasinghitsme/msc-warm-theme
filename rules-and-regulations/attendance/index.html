Rules and Regulations

Attendance

Every student is expected to be punctual and to attend school regularly. Absence from school, except for illness and other unexpected circumstances, will not be granted.

  • A student having any type of communicable disease is not allowed to attend school until certified by the doctor in writing.
  • A student who has just recovered from a contagious ailment should present a doctor's certificate of fitness to attend classes.
  • Leave may be allowed by the Principal after a written application from the parent, either through the mobile app or in writing to the school.
  • Students are expected to complete the work missed during their absence.
  • Re-examinations or re-tests will not be conducted for students who are absent during tests or examinations.
  • A half-day leave is not permitted, for security reasons. In an emergency, parents must take prior permission, come to school to collect the student and produce a valid document.
  • Students must carry the books as per the timetable and bring tiffin from home; after entering the school, the staff at the gate will not take books, tiffin or stationery to be handed over to any student.
  • 75% attendance is compulsory for students of Classes IX–XII and 80% attendance is essential for the rest. Admit card and promotion are subject to refusal if the criteria are not met as per CBSE norms.
  • Students having 100% attendance are eligible for Appreciation Certificates.
About SKS

SKS World School, Greater Noida West is an English-medium, co-educational, CBSE curriculum-based school (Affiliation No. 2134098) that runs under the aegis of the SKS Educational & Social Trust, led by its Chairman Shri S.K. Sharma.
